Sacred Tradition

The 5-Step Traditional Vedic Bilona Method

Unlike industrial ghee produced directly from high-heat milk cream (malai), authentic Vedic ghee is crafted exclusively from cultured whole milk curd churned with two-way wooden pestles.

01
🥛

Pure Gir Milk

Ethically milked from free-range indigenous Gir cows with prominent humps and Surya Ketu Nadi.

A2 Beta-Casein
02
🥣

Cultured Curd

Milk is boiled in brass/clay pots, cooled naturally, and set into whole curd using organic starter culture overnight.

Naturally Fermented
03
🪵

Bilona Churning

Bi-directional clockwise and anti-clockwise churning with wooden churners during Brahma Muhurta to separate butter.

Wooden Agitator
04
🔥

Slow Simmering

The separated Makkhan (butter) is gently simmered on mild cow dung cake flame until all moisture evaporates.

Low Thermal Impact
05
✨

Golden Nectar

Filtered through fine muslin cloth into glass jars, crystallizing into natural granular (Danedar) golden ghee.

Pristine & Danedar

⚖️ Zero Industrial Heat: Commercial ghee is extracted directly using cream centrifuges and treated with chemicals. Our Vedic method preserves vital enzymes, micronutrients, and wholesome aroma.
